Why Skaze

Built for real
creators.

Zero AI

Every photo on Skaze was taken by a human. No AI-generated images, no AI filters. Only authentic photography.

Imperfection = Art

Grainy shots, underexposed frames, blur and flaws. We don't filter them out — we celebrate them. Beauty lives in authenticity.

Photographer Community

Find others who think like you. Comment, discuss, learn from each other. No algorithms deciding what you see.

Multiple Formats

9:16, 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1 — publish in the format that fits your photo, not the platform's requirements.

Privacy First

Your photos are yours. We don't sell data to train AI. We don't use your photos for anything beyond displaying them to the community.

Photo of the Month

Chosen by the community, not an algorithm. Each month one photograph that deserves to be seen.

Our Manifesto

“Every pixel matters.
Every flaw tells a story.”

Skaze is a platform that believes real photography needs no enhancement. Imperfections are part of the message. We create a space where authenticity is a value, not a weakness.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you use an app or website.

Cookies We Use

Essential (always active)

  • session_token — maintains the logged-in user session
  • csrf_token — protection against CSRF attacks
  • cookie_consent — remembers your cookie preferences

Analytics (optional)

  • _analytics — anonymous usage statistics (you can opt out)

What We Do NOT Use

We do not use advertising, tracking, or profiling cookies. We do not share cookie data with third parties.

Managing Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings or under Settings → Privacy in the Skaze app.

Community Guidelines

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Skaze is a space for authentic photography and honest creators. The following rules help us keep this space safe and inspiring.

✓ What We Celebrate

  • Authentic photos — grainy, underexposed, imperfect, real
  • Constructive comments and meaningful discussion about photography
  • Sharing techniques, equipment and inspiration
  • Supporting other photographers, regardless of skill level

✗ What Is Prohibited

  • AI content: No images generated by AI, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion or similar tools
  • Hate speech: Content discriminating on the basis of race, gender, orientation, religion or other characteristics
  • Harassment: Personal attacks, threats, trolling
  • Spam: Mass posting, unsolicited advertising, bots
  • Privacy violations: Posting photos of others without their consent
  • Illegal content: Copyright infringement, child sexual abuse material

Content Moderation

We use a combination of user reports and manual review to moderate content. We do not use AI for moderation — decisions are made by people.

Consequences

  • First violation: warning and content removal
  • Second violation: temporary account suspension (7–30 days)
  • Serious or repeated violations: permanent account removal

Reporting Violations

Use the “Report” button on any post, or contact us: community@skaze.app
